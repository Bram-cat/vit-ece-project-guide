# VIT ECE Project Guide Context

## Product domain

VIT ECE Project Guide helps VIT Chennai ECE students choose, build, and document college projects through three chat-first work areas.

## Glossary

### Project Workspace
A saved container for one selected ECE project. It keeps the chosen idea, scores, component list, vendor links, assembly plan, research papers, record sections, and exports in one shared context across all tabs.

### Pick Project
The chat area where a student prompts first, then the AI asks one question at a time until the project goal is clear. Only after enough constraints, the AI searches or validates components, prices, vendor links, papers, and examples, then shows three compact project options.

### Build Project
The chat area where a selected Project Workspace becomes a build plan. It asks the student's current stage first: buying parts, wiring, coding, testing, or debugging. It gives the next useful step instead of dumping the full build.

### Record & Papers
The work area for VIT-format record writing and research backing. It asks what output is needed first: abstract, literature survey, diagram, references, section draft, or export plan. It generates one section at a time.

### Suitability Score
A project feasibility assessment for VIT Chennai ECE students based on ECE fit, parts availability in India, semester timeline, cost, difficulty, demo clarity, and record-writing potential.

### Professor Likeability Score
A faculty approval likelihood assessment based on novelty, ECE theory depth, research support, demo strength, seriousness, and whether the idea feels less copied.

### Component Source
A trusted place to buy ECE parts. The product uses curated vendors first, then live search. Initial vendors: Robu.in, Blinkit, Amazon India, ElectronicsComp.com, QuartzComponents, and local Chennai/Ritchie Street guidance when online options are poor.

### Purchase Guidance
The app guides students to vendor pages and warns about specs. It never purchases parts, handles payments, or checks out carts.

### VIT Record Format
The section order, wording style, diagram expectations, and reference style expected for VIT Chennai ECE records. The app supports a built-in default and uploaded sample/faculty PDF overrides.

### Research Paper Support
For each project, the app finds papers, summarizes them, explains how each paper supports the project, maps them to record sections, and cites them in VIT/IEEE style.

## Product decisions

- Default evaluation mode is VIT Chennai ECE.
- Default optimization is Safe Approval + Impressive Demo.
- YouTube and GitHub tutorials may be used for learning, but the app should discourage direct copycat submissions and suggest originality upgrades.
- The product should feel student-friendly, polished, and practical rather than like a generic dashboard.
- AI responses must be compact: one question at a time, 3 to 5 clear options, progressive disclosure, and no long dumps unless the student asks.

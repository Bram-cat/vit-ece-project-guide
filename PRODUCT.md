# PRODUCT

## Register
product

## Users
VIT Chennai ECE students preparing mini projects, course projects, lab records, and final-year project proposals.

## Product purpose
Help students move from vague project idea to approved, buildable, well-documented ECE project. The app must reduce confusion around project selection, component buying, assembling, debugging, record writing, and research paper references.

## Core experience
The app is chat-first. Students should feel like they are talking to a senior who knows VIT expectations, component availability in India, and how to make an idea professor-friendly.

## Strategic principles
- Make project approval easier without encouraging copy-paste work.
- Prefer buildable ideas over flashy impossible ideas.
- Show sources for components, datasheets, papers, and claims.
- Keep VIT record work structured, editable, and exportable.
- Guide purchases, never perform purchases.

## Tone
Clear, calm, student-friendly, practical. Avoid corporate SaaS language.

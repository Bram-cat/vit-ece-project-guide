# DESIGN

## Scene
A stressed ECE student uses this app at night before a project-review deadline, switching between laptop research, phone shopping links, and lab-record work. The interface should feel focused, warm, and reassuring.

## Direction
Product UI with an editorial lab-notebook feel. Use a light surface, ink-like text, soft circuit-board lines, and controlled accent color. Avoid generic AI dashboard cards.

## Color strategy
Restrained warm neutrals with electric teal and amber accents. Use tinted neutrals, not pure black or white.

## Typography
System sans for clarity. Use strong hierarchy and readable body line lengths.

## Interaction
Three primary tabs, each chat-centered. The selected Project Workspace is always visible so students understand that all tabs share context.
